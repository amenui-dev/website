# Materials and looseness

A material is how the second pass draws: plain, dot, halftone, ascii, print, lino, glitch, xray, chrome, liquid, collage, near. At rest they look like plain (but for the few with a look of their own); loosened, each has its own. `El::material(id)` gives a subtree another material. Scrolling fast, each material leaves its own trace (DESIGN §1.8.3).
